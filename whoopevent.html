The data behind how you perform

A wearable like WHOOP shows how your body responds, day to day. Bloodwork shows what’s driving it. In collaboration with Unilabs, every guest receives a complimentary on-site blood test measuring the markers that influence how you feel, recover, and perform, so you know where to focus next.

  • 60+BiomarkersMeasured from a single blood draw, taken privately on the day
  • 72HRSTo your resultsSent to you privately by email within 72 hours of the event
  • HormonesTestosterone, free testosterone, estradiol, FSH, LH, SHBG, cortisol
  • Heart healthApoB, lipoprotein(a), LDL, HDL, triglycerides, total cholesterol
  • MetabolismHbA1c, glucose
  • Inflammationhs-CRP, homocysteine
  • ThyroidTSH
  • Iron and bloodFerritin, iron, iron saturation, haemoglobin, full blood count
  • Liver and kidneyALT, AST, ALP, bilirubin, albumin, creatinine, eGFR
  • NutrientsVitamin D, calcium, sodium, potassium
