Meet the team

Specialists in their craft

The standard of a class is defined by the person leading it.

Clubist brings together instructors with distinct backgrounds, disciplines and perspectives, selected not only for technical expertise, but for their ability to command a room, understand movement and deliver an experience with precision.

Different methods. One standard.

Roberto

Precision at every level.

Roberto brings a depth of experience across Megaformer, strength and movement, underpinned by a background in martial arts and gymnastics. His approach is technical, disciplined and exacting, with an emphasis on understanding movement before adding intensity.

In the Megaformer Room, every detail is intentional. Tempo, resistance, transitions and form are treated with equal importance, creating sessions that demand focus as much as strength. Roberto’s teaching reflects the standard at the centre of Clubist: intelligent training, executed with precision.

Tara

Composed. Precise. Uncompromising.

Tara brings a measured and highly controlled approach to the Megaformer Room.

Her teaching is defined by clarity, attention to detail and an understanding that intensity does not need to be rushed. Each sequence is carefully built, allowing resistance and time under tension to do the work while form remains central throughout.

Tara creates an environment that feels calm but demanding. A class led with quiet authority, where progression comes through precision rather than excess.

Cristian

Discipline with purpose.

Cristian brings a focused, performance-led approach to training, combining technical coaching with a strong sense of structure.

His sessions are deliberate and progressive, placing quality of movement at the centre of the experience. Every cue has purpose, every transition is considered and intensity is built without compromising execution.

Part of the coaching team at Clubist, Cristian brings a disciplined presence to the floor and a training style centred on consistency, strength and measurable progression.

Jess

Strength through considered movement.

Jess brings energy and precision to the Mind & Body Room, with an approach that combines controlled movement with genuine muscular demand.

Her classes are defined by intelligent sequencing, strong technical cues and an ability to build intensity without losing refinement. Expect sustained work, deliberate transitions and programming that asks for focus from beginning to end.

Jess brings an athletic quality to Pilates and sculpt, creating sessions that feel fluid in their delivery and uncompromising in their execution.

Lexie

Los Angeles influence. A method of her own.

Lexie’s approach to movement has been shaped by years spent teaching and travelling between Los Angeles, Dubai, Turkey, Qatar and Spain, with LA playing a defining role in the evolution of her contemporary sculpt style.

Her method brings together Pilates-based sculpt, functional strength, rhythmic movement and controlled resistance, balancing precision with fluidity and intensity with connection.

At Clubist, Lexie translates that international perspective into programming that feels current, intelligent and distinctive. Movement is purposeful, sequencing is considered and every class carries a clear point of view.

Molly

Intelligent movement, intuitively delivered.

Molly brings a refined and attentive approach to both the Mind & Body Room and Megaformer Room.

Her teaching balances technical precision with an instinctive understanding of how the body moves, allowing classes to build progressively without ever feeling forced. Controlled sequencing, considered resistance and clear instruction form the foundation of her approach.

There is strength in the detail. Molly’s sessions are composed, challenging and designed to leave members with a greater understanding of their movement as well as the work itself.

Chad

Energy, with structure.

Chad specialises in personal training, group fitness, boxing and HIIT, with experience across Barry’s Qatar, OX Fitness Lab and Platform Studios. His coaching style combines high energy with deliberate programming, placing structure, safety and progression at the centre of every session.

In the Dark Room, that translates into a class that moves with pace but never without purpose. Functional strength, progressive running and conditioning are brought together through precise programming and a strong command of the room.

Intensity is expected. Progress is the objective.

Keelan

Performance without distraction.

Keelan brings a direct and disciplined approach to strength and conditioning.

His programming is centred on the fundamentals that matter: quality movement, progressive load, cardiovascular capacity and consistency. Sessions are structured to challenge performance while maintaining a clear technical standard throughout.

In the Dark Room, Keelan’s coaching cuts through unnecessary complexity. The objective is simple: move well, work with intent and build the capacity to perform at a higher level over time.
