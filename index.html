Introduction

Built for those who seek more than a workout

Clubist is a wellness club, the meeting point of movement, community and lifestyle in the heart of Dubai Marina. Not just a studio, but the anchor of a new kind of wellness culture.

A place to arrive, to reset, to belong.

Every class is designed with intention, every space shaped for flow. Spa-style amenities, intelligent programming and the calibre of its instructors are all carefully curated so that performance and calm can exist side by side.

Three studios. One club.

The Studios

Three studios. Three distinct disciplines.

Each studio within Clubist has its own identity, purpose and atmosphere. Together, they allow movement to change with the needs of the day.

The Mind & Body Room

Movement with intention

The centre of mindful movement, a state of the art infrared studio dedicated to Pilates, barre, sculpt and strength. The room is heated to 32°C by infrared panels whose gentle, penetrating warmth heats the body directly, supporting mobility, circulation and depth of practice.

Soft textures, natural tones and considered light create a calm yet focused setting, allowing members to move with clarity and control.

Infra Form

Precision, strengthened by heat.

A considered approach to mat Pilates, combining controlled movement, sustained muscular engagement and infrared heat. Infra Form develops strength, stability and body awareness through intelligent sequencing, with every movement performed with purpose. The 32°C infrared warmth readies the muscles for deeper, more precise work.

Book this class
Infra Sequence

Sculpt, refined.

A progressive full-body sequence built around repetition, control and muscular endurance. Infra Sequence moves with intention, using infrared heat to heighten the experience while precise programming creates strength, definition and stamina. Infrared heat sustains muscle warmth through every repetition.

Book this class
Infra Barre

Small movement. Exacting work.

A disciplined Barre practice centred on control, alignment and sustained muscular engagement. Precise, low-impact sequences challenge stability and endurance, creating an intensity that builds gradually and remains long after the movement ends. Infrared warmth keeps muscles supple through the longest holds.

Book this class
Infra Shred

Strength under pressure.

The most performance-led expression of the Mind & Body Room. Infra Shred combines resistance, conditioning and infrared heat in a structured session designed to challenge strength and stamina while maintaining precision throughout. The 32°C room raises the demand on every interval.

Book this class
Infra Sculpt & Flow

Strength in motion.

A considered balance of sculpt and flow, moving seamlessly between muscular challenge and mobility. Controlled sequences build strength and endurance while fluid transitions create a practice that feels both powerful and composed. Warmth carries the body further into each range of motion.

Book this class
Infra Restore & Reset

Recovery, with purpose.

A restorative practice designed to create space in the body and quiet in the mind. Mobility, controlled stretching and measured movement work together within the infrared environment to release tension, support recovery and restore balance. Infrared heat supports circulation and helps the body release.

The Megaformer Room

Strength through control

Dedicated to the Lagree method, with Megaformers designed for intelligent, results-driven training. Every element, from the placement of each bed to the balance of lighting and sound, has been engineered to heighten focus and form.

A unique combination of low-impact yet high-intensity movement, challenging strength, endurance and stability in equal measure.

Megaformer

Strength, refined through control.

Clubist’s signature Lagree experience. Slow, deliberate movement combines with continuous resistance and sustained time under tension to challenge strength, stability and endurance. Every transition matters. Every movement is executed with precision.

Book this class
Megaformer Light

The method, introduced with precision.

A more measured introduction to Lagree, created to establish confidence on the Megaformer without compromising the integrity of the method. Additional time is given to technique, transitions and control, allowing strength to develop through a deeper understanding of the movement.

The Dark Room

Performance, intensified

The home of intensity, designed for both cardio-driven and strength-focused training. State-of-the-art lighting and sound create an immersive atmosphere where focus sharpens and energy rises, turning each session into an experience as precise as it is powerful.

Not just a room for exertion, but a modern arena of movement, where limits are tested, ambition is fuelled and resilience is built with every session.

HIIT & Run

Conditioning at full pace.

A high-performance combination of treadmill intervals and strength training. HIIT & Run moves between speed, resistance and controlled recovery, using intelligent programming to challenge cardiovascular capacity, strength and endurance within one immersive session.

Lift & Load

Strength, without distraction.

A focused resistance session built around quality movement, progressive load and purposeful programming. Lift & Load places technique first, creating the foundations for measurable strength and long-term progression.

Thirtyfour

The natural complement

Clubist was conceived as the anchor, a wellness club focused on training, performance and physical wellbeing. Thirtyfour, its sister brand, completes the lifestyle ecosystem: a place to pause, refuel and reconnect.

Where Clubist ignites movement and ambition, Thirtyfour provides nourishment and grounding. Though distinct in identity, both share the same ethos: quiet sophistication, thoughtful detail and authentic community.

Community

A club built on belonging

Clubist is more than a place to train. It is a club built on connection, shared purpose and belonging. First-time guests move alongside long-standing members, and instructors guide with both expertise and care.

With every session, every exchange and every collaboration, the Clubist network deepens. Not defined by exclusivity, but by a collective pursuit of growth and a sense of community that endures beyond the studio walls.

Amenities

From arrival to departure

Every detail at Clubist is designed to support performance while reflecting the ease and refinement of a wellness club. Amenities are not additions, but part of the experience.

On arrival

Valet

Your time begins the moment you arrive. With dedicated valet service at Marina Quays, entry is discreet, efficient and without interruption.

Between sets

Hydration

Filtered water fountains are placed throughout the venue, ensuring members remain refreshed and focused at every stage of their training.

After class

Showers & changing

Plush towels and spa-style showers are available after every class, allowing members to reset with comfort and continue their day with ease.

Beyond the club

Retail

A small curated selection of essentials, chosen with the same intent as our training. From performance wear to grip socks, each piece extends the identity of Clubist into life beyond the studio.

Instructors

They are its heartbeat

The instructors are the foundation of Clubist. More than coaches, they are specialists in their craft, setting the standard for movement, technique and experience within the club.

Each has been selected not only for expertise, but for presence: the ability to lead with authority, guide with care, and create an environment where progress feels both attainable and elevated.
